## [](http://discourse.ros.org/t/ros-news-of-the-week-for-august-31st-2026/57868/print#p-117599-ros-news-of-the-week-for-august-31st-2026-1)ROS News of the Week for August 31st, 2026

A big thank you to our colleagues at Bosch for hosting ROS By-The-Bay last night! Last night was our last local ROS event before ROSCon Global in Toronto.

If you are attending [ROSCon Global in Toronto](https://roscon.ros.org/2026/) you should have received an email that details all of our amazing after hours events including a mixer on workshop day, a bus tour of the Waterloo robotics ecosystem, and our post-ROSCon hackathon.

![Image 1: followme](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/f/e/fe1d0a7bc6909847cfadc33dc1066290fc32d40b.gif)

[Fares Espiro](https://github.com/EspiroFares), a grad student in Sweden, just released the source code for his grad work on an indoor following robot with a full simulation stack based on Gazebo and ROS 2. [It is definitely worth a look.](https://github.com/EspiroFares/Autonomous-UAV)

![Image 2: bimo](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/9/7/973effa8bfcd3f124c4f138c673c5f185faa1ef5.webp)

 Another open source biped robot has entered the chat. This one is called Bimo and you [can learn more here.](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/bimo-an-open-source-bipedal-robotics-kit-with-sim-to-real-transfer/57846) We’re not quite at the pre-orders phase of the project, but this looks pretty cool.

![Image 3: rmf](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/4/4/4483403adb5211e91fb402706f999c267b03f500.gif)

 Most of our summer Google Summer of Code students wrapped up this week. Two of the students posted final reports this week that are worth your attention:

*   [@mo_mo](http://discourse.ros.org/u/mo_mo) did a lot of work on [quality assurance of Gazebo reset of core systems.](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/gsoc-2026-qa-of-reset-of-core-systems/57775/2) RL training in Gazebo should run a whole lot smoother now.
*   [@ArizmendiWan](http://discourse.ros.org/u/arizmendiwan) has a suite of [UI/UX improvements for the Open-RMF Crossflow diagram editor](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/ui-ux-improvements-to-the-crossflow-diagram-editor/57838).

![Image 4: :tada:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/tada.png?v=15)_I want to extend a giant thank you to all of our wonderful students who spent their summer supporting our open source projects!_
