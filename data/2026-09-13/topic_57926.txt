We’re planning a Jazzy Jalisco sync next Wednesday September 9, 2026 (Singapore). There are currently [882 packages](http://repo.ros2.org/status_page/ros_jazzy_default.html?q=SYNC)ready to sync and [one regression](http://repo.ros2.org/status_page/ros_jazzy_default.html?q=REGRESSION) that we hope to fix.

If you find any other regression when testing, please report it upstream and leave a comment here so we can track it leading up to the sync.

Starting today September 7, 2026 (Singapore) we’ll hold all rosdistro PRs unless they fix reported regressions.
