[mjcarroll](http://discourse.ros.org/u/mjcarroll) OSRA TGC Member 

[4d](http://discourse.ros.org/t/new-ros-pmc-members/57984 "Post date")

I am happy to announce that as of the [ROS PMC Meeting on September 8th](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/ros-pmc-minutes-for-september-8-2026/57957), the ROS PMC is adding three additional members to the project management committee per the [ROS Project Charter](https://osralliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/ros_project_charter.pdf).

Please join me in welcoming:

[@skye.galaxy](http://discourse.ros.org/u/skye.galaxy) of [Polymath Robotics](https://www.polymathrobotics.com/) - Skyler has heavily involved in the Client Libraries working group, most recently working on landing the CBG events executor in Lyrical, benchmarking it, and moving it towards the default in future distributions. Look for her presentation at ROSCon 2026!

[@JM_ROS](http://discourse.ros.org/u/jm_ros) of [Cellumation](https://cellumation.com/) - Janosch has also been heavily involved in the Client Libraries working group, contributing not only the new events executor implementation as well as performance improvements across the stack. Look for his presentation at ROSCon 2026!

[@Andrew_Symington](http://discourse.ros.org/u/andrew_symington) of [Intrinsic](https://www.intrinsic.ai/) - Andrew has worked on implementing the ROS Central Registry set of bazel build rules for the ROS ecosystem. Look for his presentation at ROSCon 2026!

Thank you for all of your contributions so far, and I’m excited to see what you do as PMC members!
