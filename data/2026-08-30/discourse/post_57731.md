[2d](https://discourse.ros.org/t/homebrew-dropping-support-for-macos-intel-amd64/57731 "Post date")

Hi all, homebrew announced last year that they were planning to drop support for intel / amd64 CPUs in macOS in September 2026. I opened an [issue](https://github.com/gazebosim/docs/issues/602#issuecomment-3445053712 "https://github.com/gazebosim/docs/issues/602#issuecomment-3445053712") with details about this change and a proposed plan, and since brew has already stopped building new intel bottles, I’m announcing it here. My apologies for not announcing it in a discoverable way sooner.

My plan is to stop running CI and building bottles for intel / amd64 CPUs on macOS in Gazebo, to follow the brew policy. See [gazebosim/docs#602](https://github.com/gazebosim/docs/issues/602#issuecomment-3445053712 "https://github.com/gazebosim/docs/issues/602#issuecomment-3445053712") for more details.
