## [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/x/57728#p-117369-ros-news-for-the-week-of-august-24th-2026-1)ROS News for the Week of August 24th, 2026

I ran into [@01binary](https://discourse.ros.org/u/01binary) and his Str1ker mech robot at Open Sauce and Teardown. Quite frankly, it is one of the most over-the-top ROS personal projects I’ve ever seen. [This week the source code and CAD files dropped so you can take a peek under the hood.](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/open-source-mech-integrated-with-ros-noetic/57645/3)

We’re about three months out from the Lyrical Release and the ROS package ecosystem is starting to catch up. This week [@smac](https://discourse.ros.org/u/smac) released [Nav2 for Lyrical!](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/nav2-lyrical-release-key-features/57666) There’s a host of new features and it is worth checking out the release notes.

![Image 1: smolvla](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/b/d/bdf83fe250712e23e6a98877035dac0db1384ab3.gif)

This week a team in Germany released [ROS2SmolVLA a ROS package that enables small vision-language-action models with industrial-grade cobots running ROS 2.](https://una-auxme.github.io/en/projects/ros2smolvla/) I had a lot of great conversations this week about “small models” for robot deployment and this seems to scratch that itch.

![Image 2: IBVS-admittance](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/5/7/573009b682e2fd719583c2b710540b6dabbdbe5c.gif)

![Image 3: IBVS-rot](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/0/6/06071777aac96109bc3f99a2f30310d49b702345.gif)

![Image 4: IBVS-trans](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/2/5/25f32cb81eb5f9d5aa141d3399cb6145b71b79cf.gif)

[@aitor-ibarguren](https://discourse.ros.org/u/aitor-ibarguren) just released a set of [ROS 2 visual servoing controllers](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/announcement-ros2-visual-servoing-controllers/57661) for ROS 2 Jazzy that includes both a position-based visual servoing (PBVS) and an image-based visual servoing controller (IBVS). The controllers include an action server providing a parametrizable PBVS/IBVS task, mimicking the joint trajectory controller’s functionality.

![Image 5: autodock](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/1/f/1f194f06eed0b5b15c4f3b4819353c523d3f70fd.webp)

Our colleagues over at Hello Robot just published a blog post on building [autodocking for the Stretch 4.](https://binitshah.github.io/blog/stretch-4-autodocking/)

Our colleagues in Japan [just released this adorable 3D printed mini ROS 2 quadruped.](https://www.printpupper.com/)
