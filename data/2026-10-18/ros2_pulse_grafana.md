Open Robotics has a survey asking for _your_ feedback. Fill it in to enter a prize draw!

[Open Robotics](https://www.openrobotics.org/) works to support the development, distribution, and adoption of open-source software for use in robotics research, education, and product development. As an organisation and as a movement, we know a lot about the software, tools and standards that we build, but _we do not know enough about the people who use them_ in their work, in their hobbies, and in their studies.

What motivates people to use the Open Robotics Suite (ROS, ros-controls, Space ROS, Gazebo, Open-RMF, and the infrastructure that supports them), in whole or in part? What motivates companies to make their products depend on our software? What features are actually relied upon every day, and which are no longer necessary? Or, conversely, what motivates someone to _not_ use the Open Robotics Suite? To begin answering these questions, we are starting a new annual survey of everyone who is or could be using our software.

We are asking anyone and everyone who is in robotics, in any way, to answer this survey. Even if you do not use any Open Robotics software, or you think that ROS is not suitable for your work, or you’ve only just started learning Gazebo, or you tried and have abandoned ROS, we still want to hear from you. If you’ve never used ROS, Gazebo, or any of our other software, we want to hear from you!

Achieving our mission requires knowing who we are or should be building software for, no matter who they are or where they use it. This survey is how we get to know our community better.

**You can fill in the survey by following this link**: [https://go.openrobotics.org/orsurvey26](https://go.openrobotics.org/orsurvey26)

Some updates on ros2_pulse since the first post ([Ros2_pulse: per-topic rates and node liveness, including intra-process, on stock binaries](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/57637)), the LD_PRELOAD probe that counts per-topic rates and node liveness from the tracetools hooks instead of subscribing.

**Python nodes are counted on both sides now.** Before, a Python subscriber’s deliveries never showed up, because rclpy isn’t instrumented. The probe now also hooks the `rmw_take` tracepoint, so rclpy (and rclc) receives count too. rclcpp still counts at `callback_start`, so nothing is counted twice. Needs Cyclone or Fast DDS, or Connext from Jazzy.

**Loaned messages.** At the tracepoint level a loan looks like a normal publish, or on Humble like nothing at all. The probe now wraps `rcl_publish_loaned_message` / `rcl_take_loaned_message` and prints a separate loaned rate per topic. Worth knowing: from Jazzy, Fast DDS loans any plain type by default, so a Float64 publisher may be going through loans without you asking. Came out of a question by @armaho on rclcpp#3153.

**Grafana.**`pulse-export` serves the probe’s windows as Prometheus metrics on :9464 and can push OTLP/HTTP. The screenshot above is a live run on my laptop, one camera topic drops under its 25 Hz floor and the warn lights up. Docker compose with Prometheus, Grafana and the dashboard: [ros2_pulse/examples/grafana at main · TanayK07/ros2_pulse · GitHub](https://github.com/TanayK07/ros2_pulse/tree/main/examples/grafana)

**Smaller things.** The startup banner prints the tracetools version and warns on one CI doesn’t run (thanks [@christophebedard](https://discourse.ros.org/u/christophebedard) for the stability answer). pulse-top reads the default text log and has `--theme light`, thanks to [@peci1![Image 10: czechia](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/czechia.png?v=15)](https://discourse.ros.org/u/peci1) trying it on Kilted. A process frozen with SIGSTOP now shows the real gap after it resumes.

30 s explainer on why `ros2 topic hz` costs CPU on intra-process topics: [https://youtu.be/YH8w2bI2EjM](https://youtu.be/YH8w2bI2EjM)

Install: `sudo apt install ros-$ROS_DISTRO-ros2-pulse` (Humble, Jazzy, Kilted). The rclpy and loan counting are in 0.6.0, which reaches apt with the next sync, until then build from main. Code: [GitHub - TanayK07/ros2_pulse: Near-zero-overhead ROS 2 topic/node frequency probe (inter + intra process) via an LD_PRELOAD tracetools shim. · GitHub](https://github.com/TanayK07/ros2_pulse)

Feedback welcome, especially if it breaks on your setup.
