## [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/58167#p-118100-ros-pmc-business-1)ROS PMC Business

*   ![Image 1: :white_check_mark:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/white_check_mark.png?v=15)**Vote result: RHEL to Tier 1 on Rolling, Fedora to Tier 3. PASSES.** The vote closed Friday. 
    *   Documentation has already been updated.

*   **REP 158 vote in progress.** Most ROS PMC votes are in. The Gazebo PMC is also listed on this REP, so [@mjcarroll](https://discourse.ros.org/u/mjcarroll) attended their meeting on Monday and put forward a motion for them to vote as well. **The Gazebo vote closes Friday.**
    *   Process note worth knowing: across all affected PMCs, quorum is 50% of members, and the outcome is a simple majority of those who vote. In principle 25% of the combined ROS and Gazebo membership could carry an acceptance. That is a lower bar than it first appears.
    *   On passing, REP 158 moves to **Accepted** rather than Finalized. The authors then complete the implementation, after which it can be finalized.

*   **Committer vote going out this afternoon**, ahead of ROSCon. No objections were raised to the proposed scopes. 
    *   All committers get access to `ros2_documentation` and the ability to run CI.
    *   Timple: `geometry`, `tf2`, `launch`
    *   Nadav Elkabets: `rclpy`
    *   [@KimMcG](https://discourse.ros.org/u/kimmcg): `ros2/ros2`, `ci`, `setup-ros`, `action-ros-ci`
    *   Martin Pecka: `point_cloud_transport`, with room to expand over time
    *   @nvcyc: `rosidl_buffer_backends`, `rosidl*`, `rmw*`, largely formalizing access he already has
    *   Leonardo Rossetti: `ros2/ros2`, `ros2/ci`
    *   **Any committer is immediately eligible to be a rosdistro reviewer** if they want to be. [@cottsay](https://discourse.ros.org/u/cottsay) noted it would be good to have more reviewers with Red Hat context.
    *   The general principle: adding committers should be cheap. It is easy to add them and, as we recently established, considerably easier to remove a committer than a PMC member.

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/58167#p-118100-buildfarm-update-3)Buildfarm update

The buildfarm builds and tests ROS packages across all supported distributions and platforms. This section covers new issues, current priorities, and infrastructure changes that affect maintainers.

*   ![Image 3: :new_button:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/new_button.png?v=15) Issues 
    *   [`launch_testing_examples.check_msgs_launch_test` fails to receive messages on the chatter topic](https://github.com/ros2/examples/issues/455). Consistent, and notably fails on Zenoh and Cyclone but not Fast DDS. [@emersonknapp](https://discourse.ros.org/u/emersonknapp) observed that Fast DDS currently has much faster initial topic discovery than the default configurations of the other two, so this may be a discovery timing problem with the test not waiting properly. Assigned to [@yadunund](https://discourse.ros.org/u/yadunund).
    *   [Windows packaging build regression from `rosbag2_performance_benchmarking` hitting the MSVC object-file section limit](https://github.com/ros2/rosbag2/issues/2497). A fix is up but not merged, having failed on what appears to be a flaky runner. The fix is the big object flag.
    *   [Flaky async node, service, and client tests timing out after 120 seconds](https://github.com/ros2/rclpy/issues/1719). To be flagged to Nadav Elkabets, who worked on the asyncio executor and found a deadlock while preparing his ROSCon presentation.
    *   [`test_load_node_with_param_file` intermittently hangs while loading composable nodes](https://github.com/ros2/launch_ros/issues/572). [@mjcarroll](https://discourse.ros.org/u/mjcarroll) will take a look.
    *   [Flaky `test_tf2.test_message_filter.gtest.missing_result` timeout on Windows with `rmw_zenoh_cpp`](https://github.com/ros2/rmw_zenoh/issues/1054). First failure within the last 15 days, with logs already aged out. To be picked up by someone with a Windows setup, available later this week.
    *   [`benchmark_executor` tests consistently timeout on Ubuntu Resolute AMD64](https://github.com/ros2/rclcpp/issues/3273). The benchmark ramps iteration count based on sample timing, so a hang partway through is suspicious. [@skye.galaxy](https://discourse.ros.org/u/skye.galaxy) suspects it is locking up in the multi-threaded executor and never getting past that stage. [@jmachowinski](https://discourse.ros.org/u/jmachowinski) noted that on a 20-core machine the multi-threaded test takes a very long time, though a four-core worker should be fine. Worth temporarily raising the timeout to establish whether it is hung or merely slow. **[@jmachowinski](https://discourse.ros.org/u/jmachowinski) and [@skye.galaxy](https://discourse.ros.org/u/skye.galaxy) will do a triage pass** to characterize the problem, without committing to a fix.
