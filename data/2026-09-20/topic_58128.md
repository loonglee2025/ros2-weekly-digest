There have been several new and updated packages in Kilted since it was released, and it’s time to sync those new features and fixes to the main apt and dnf repositories.

There are currently [no regressions](http://repo.ros2.org/status_page/ros_kilted_default.html?q=REGRESSION) and [257 packages](http://repo.ros2.org/status_page/ros_kilted_default.html?q=SYNC) to release.

We will start holding rosdistro PRs for the sync on September 15, 2026 (Los Angeles), with a plan to do the sync on September 16, 2026 (Los Angeles).
