Next week is [Foxglove’s Actuate conference](https://actuate.foxglove.dev/?utm_campaign=40976810-Actuate&utm_content=374017076&utm_medium=social&utm_source=linkedin&hss_channel=lcp-72660627) in San Francisco. ![Image 6: kiwiseg](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/b/0/b03c51220503782e29a197a5e53e019707249cd3.webp)

Our friends at [Robot.com](http://robot.com/) (formerly Kiwibot) have released [semantic_segmentation_layer, a Nav2 costmap layer for semantic segmentation](https://github.com/kiwicampus/semantic_segmentation_layer). If you are looking to add additional information to your Nav2 stack this looks like a really good production-ready solution.

![Image 7: watti2](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/d/5/d53ccd8ee6a2f05893c99f08071a39009214c769.webp)

[Watti](https://github.com/Nikolay-Tyulkin/Watti) is an animatronic lamp reminiscent of the Pixar lamp. The source code hasn’t been released yet but I’m told it runs ROS 2. What I find really interesting is that this work dovetails with the work of the [Input Inc.](https://input-inc.com/) who spoke at our ROS By-The-Bay meetup last night. Both groups are working on tools that make it much easier to build interactive robots and animitronic displays. _Perhaps we need a new animatronics community group?_

[![Image 8: image](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/optimized/3X/d/8/d88146df6ea16b785fbfe1153cca16376341f53f_2_517x250.png)](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/d/8/d88146df6ea16b785fbfe1153cca16376341f53f.png "image")

I love parametric design. My CAD skills aren’t great, so I often build small 3D printing projects using [OpenSCAD](https://openscad.org/). This week I came across this awesome parametric 3D-printed robot gripper configurator called [Fingermod](https://fingermod.steph.ng/). It lets you choose from a variety of designs and configure them via a friendly web-ui. Maybe someone could vibe code a nice OpenSCAD to web-UI configurator tool to make building robot tools like this easier.

![Image 9: feetech](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/3/0/306293fe7c3cf1ef6b991b69ac9b350651b9fa6b.webp)

Have a cheap Feetech servo? [NORI MotorLab](https://github.com/Nori-Robotics/feetech-motorlab) is a browser-based workspace for testing and characterizing 12 V Feetech STS3215, STS3250, and STS3095 servos. MotorLab provides multi-servo telemetry, individual and bus-wide motion controls, live tuning, recording, encoder centering, EEPROM configuration, and a guarded motor-ID station.
