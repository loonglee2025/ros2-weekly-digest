![Image 3: :canada:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/canada.png?v=15) ROSCon Global 2026 was last week in Toronto! There were a lot of big announcements, including Intrinsic releasing [Intrinsic Open Core](https://www.intrinsic.ai/intrinsic-core) and [wrapping up the AI for Industry challenge](https://www.intrinsic.ai/blog/posts/ai-for-industry-challenge), [PickNik and Qualcomm merging](https://osralliance.org/2026/09/open-robotics-celebrates-the-union-of-two-founding-osra-members/), [Red Hat Enterprise Linux being elevated to a Tier 1 ROS operating system](https://osralliance.org/2026/09/osra-member-red-hats-red-hat-enterprise-linux-is-now-a-supported-platform-for-ros/), and [NVIDIA releasing Isaac ROS 5](https://osralliance.org/2026/09/isaac-5-0-nvidias-ros-distribution-brings-speed-and-collaboration/). In other news, ROSCon Global 2027 will be held in ![Image 4: :poland:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/poland.png?v=15) Krakow, Poland . Videos from ROSCon 2026 should be out in a couple of weeks.

  

[![Image 5: AnnualSurvey (1)](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/optimized/3X/e/5/e55adcfad2576d80f5ef30ca24705a19d9c870ec_2_375x375.jpeg)](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/e/5/e55adcfad2576d80f5ef30ca24705a19d9c870ec.jpeg "AnnualSurvey (1)")

[On the heels of ROSCon we’re running our annual developer survey.](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/complete-the-open-robotics-annual-survey-2026-to-enter-the-prize-draw/58488)_We do not collect telemetry from the users of our open source projects, and this is the best mechanism we have for getting feedback on our open source projects._ The good news is that we’re giving away ![Image 6: :five:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/five.png?v=15)![Image 7: :zero:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/zero.png?v=15) ROS or Gazebo shirts to survey participants chosen at random. If you make the time to take the survey there’s a pretty good chance you’ll get some free OSRF swag.

  

[![Image 8: Orion_Thumbnail](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/optimized/3X/5/b/5b3b0fced5267b02aa73a6a7475d2e1e7ec525de_2_517x291.jpeg)](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/5/b/5b3b0fced5267b02aa73a6a7475d2e1e7ec525de.jpeg "Orion_Thumbnail")

[Orion is an open source 12 degree of freedom mini quadruped robot that runs ROS.](https://github.com/AshishA26/Orion-Quadruped). Unlike a lot of the other mini-quads out there it has a pair of stereo cameras, a LIDAR, and an NVIDIA Jetson so it can run a full Nav2 autonomy stack.

![Image 9: cpr](https://us1.discourse-cdn.com/flex022/uploads/ros/original/3X/6/6/6657781a20d5bcd54bdf96113c13c23687cb8f02.webp)

  
 Are you struggling to properly configure your ROS middleware layer? Our colleagues at Clearpath have open sourced their [ROSCon Global 2026 Workshop on Mastering the Jazzy RMW](https://github.com/clearpathrobotics/roscon2026-mastering-the-jazzy-rmw). Clearpath configures a lot of robots, and they’ve distilled their knowledge down to an easy-to-follow self-guided workshop that will teach you the ins and outs of RMW configuration.
