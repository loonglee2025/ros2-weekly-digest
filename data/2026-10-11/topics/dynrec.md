Open Robotics has a survey asking for _your_ feedback. Fill it in to enter a prize draw!

[Open Robotics](https://www.openrobotics.org/) works to support the development, distribution, and adoption of open-source software for use in robotics research, education, and product development. As an organisation and as a movement, we know a lot about the software, tools and standards that we build, but _we do not know enough about the people who use them_ in their work, in their hobbies, and in their studies.

What motivates people to use the Open Robotics Suite (ROS, ros-controls, Space ROS, Gazebo, Open-RMF, and the infrastructure that supports them), in whole or in part? What motivates companies to make their products depend on our software? What features are actually relied upon every day, and which are no longer necessary? Or, conversely, what motivates someone to _not_ use the Open Robotics Suite? To begin answering these questions, we are starting a new annual survey of everyone who is or could be using our software.

We are asking anyone and everyone who is in robotics, in any way, to answer this survey. Even if you do not use any Open Robotics software, or you think that ROS is not suitable for your work, or you’ve only just started learning Gazebo, or you tried and have abandoned ROS, we still want to hear from you. If you’ve never used ROS, Gazebo, or any of our other software, we want to hear from you!

Achieving our mission requires knowing who we are or should be building software for, no matter who they are or where they use it. This survey is how we get to know our community better.

**You can fill in the survey by following this link**: [https://go.openrobotics.org/orsurvey26](https://go.openrobotics.org/orsurvey26)

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/rosbag2-dynamic-recorder-dynrec/58192#p-118556-prize-draw-1)Prize draw

All survey participants will have the opportunity to enter a prize draw to win one item of their choice from the [Open Robotics Store](https://store.openrobotics.org/). At the end of the survey you will have the opportunity to enter your email address for the purposes of entering this prize draw. You must enter a valid email address to be eligible for the prize draw. **50 participants will be chosen at random** from those who enter.

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/rosbag2-dynamic-recorder-dynrec/58192#p-118556-deadline-2)Deadline

**The survey period ends on the 30th of November, 2026**. All _completed_ surveys received by this date will be eligible for the prize draw.

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/rosbag2-dynamic-recorder-dynrec/58192#p-118556-who-should-fill-in-the-survey-3)Who should fill in the survey?

_Everyone_! We want the opinions of everyone working in, studying in, and playing in robotics. We want the opinions of every individual - not just one response per company. Get your colleagues to fill it in, your team mates, your staff, your classmates. The more information we have, the better we can work to ensure that the software you depend on better meets your needs - or, if you don’t use it, gets to the point where you want to!

Earlier this year I had to prepare a ROS2 course for undergraduate students as part of my work, where I had to debug a series of robots over different exercises where topics often changed, which made rosbag2 debugging rather tedious to set up each time.

For this reason, I am currently developing **dynrec** (short for rosbag2 Dynamic Recorder): a package designed to allow users to dynamically select which topics to record during a rosbag2 run. This means that you could now freely add and remove topics during recording, alternate between topic sets, or pause and resume, all without having to stop the recording process.

Feedback is greatly appreciated, as it helps me improve the project, especially on the early stages.

Link to the code can be found here: [GitHub - juandanielsg/rosbag2_dynamic_recorder: A rosbag2 recorder that changes its recorded topic set at runtime, without stopping the writer or splitting the bag. · GitHub](https://github.com/juandanielsg/rosbag2_dynamic_recorder)  
 Link to the project’s page: [rosbag2_dynamic_recorder · juandanielsg.eu](https://juandanielsg.eu/en/projects/rosbag2-dynamic-recorder/)

Great question and thank you very much for your comment! So far, the resulting file is identical to the rosbag2 bags in terms of format and tool compatibility (they can be replayed via the standard ros2 bag play or checked via ros2 bag info, although the package offers its own info command).

On the other hand, there are some particularities on the dynamically recorded bags:

*   There are event topics which signal at which point a topic was added, removed or recording was paused and resumed. These are there so that you can tell whether missing topics are a result of the dynamic recorder’s instructions or due to external factors.
*   The bags don’t have all the features from the original rosbag2 packages at the moment. Features like compression are missing, but will be added soon.

𝐝𝐲𝐧𝐫𝐞𝐜 𝐯0.2.0 𝐢𝐬 𝐨𝐮𝐭!

This is the first noticeable update since the first announcement of the project, and it comes with some new features and changes:

𝐍𝐨𝐯𝐞𝐥𝐭𝐢𝐞𝐬:

*   𝐃𝐢𝐬𝐤 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐬𝐢𝐳𝐞 𝐠𝐮𝐚𝐫𝐝𝐬: recording stops when free disk space falls below min_free_space / min_free_space_percent, or when the whole bag passes max_bag_size. Each stop is explained by an event written into the bag.
*   𝐒𝐢𝐦𝐮𝐥𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧 𝐭𝐢𝐦𝐞: with use_sim_time, messages and schedules use the simulation clock, and the recorder waits for /clock before opening the bag.
*   𝐁𝐞𝐭𝐭𝐞𝐫 𝐥𝐨𝐬𝐬 𝐫𝐞𝐩𝐨𝐫𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠: losses are now broken down per topic in the status.
*   𝐌𝐨𝐫𝐞 𝐢𝐧 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐬𝐭𝐚𝐭𝐮𝐬: pending schedules are listed, and each file split is recorded as a timestamped event.
*   𝐒𝐭𝐚𝐠𝐞𝐝 𝐰𝐫𝐢𝐭𝐞𝐬: slow storage operations no longer block incoming messages.
*   𝐒𝐡𝐚𝐫𝐞𝐝 𝐜𝐥𝐢𝐞𝐧𝐭: the CLI and the web UI both use the _dynrec.Recorder_ Python library now.

𝐂𝐡𝐚𝐧𝐠𝐞𝐬:

*   𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐫𝐞𝐜𝐨𝐫𝐝𝐞𝐫 𝐧𝐨𝐝𝐞 𝐢𝐬 𝐬𝐩𝐥𝐢𝐭 𝐢𝐧𝐭𝐨 𝐬𝐞𝐩𝐚𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐞 𝐩𝐚𝐫𝐭𝐬: Bag, Scheduler, StorageGuard, LossAccounting and config.
*   𝐃𝐮𝐩𝐥𝐢𝐜𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐝 𝐜𝐨𝐝𝐞 𝐫𝐞𝐦𝐨𝐯𝐞𝐝.
*   𝐄𝐚𝐜𝐡 𝐠𝐫𝐨𝐮𝐩 𝐨𝐟 𝐜𝐚𝐥𝐥𝐛𝐚𝐜𝐤𝐬 𝐧𝐨𝐰 𝐫𝐮𝐧𝐬 𝐨𝐧 𝐢𝐭𝐬 𝐨𝐰𝐧 𝐭𝐡𝐫𝐞𝐚𝐝.
*   𝐄𝐥𝐚𝐩𝐬𝐞𝐝 𝐭𝐢𝐦𝐞 𝐬𝐭𝐨𝐩𝐬 𝐜𝐨𝐮𝐧𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐚𝐭 𝐚 𝐬𝐭𝐨𝐩, and the status is stamped with the recorders clock.
*   𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐔𝐈𝐬 𝐥𝐚𝐮𝐧𝐜𝐡 𝐟𝐢𝐥𝐞 𝐧𝐨𝐰 𝐢𝐧𝐜𝐥𝐮𝐝𝐞𝐬 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐫𝐞𝐜𝐨𝐫𝐝𝐞𝐫𝐬, so the two share their defaults.

I have also received some comments and private messages asking why I haven’t proposed this as a rosbag2 pull request. I would love to directly contribute to the ROS2 official package list, but I would like more feedback and discussion around the package and the features before even considering to do that.
