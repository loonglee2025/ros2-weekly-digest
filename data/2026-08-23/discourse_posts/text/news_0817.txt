 In the lead up to ROSCon we have a bunch of fantastic events lined up in the Bay Area and online. Next Tuesday in San Francisco we’re holding a Trossen X ROS Industry Night , on Wednesday we have our August Gazebo Community Meeting on Gym-Gazebo-Sim, a basic toolkit for using Gymnasium API in Gazebo , and the Ruggedized agricultural technology and robotics conference will be held in Salinas on Wednesday and Thursday (I’ll be there giving a talk). The following week we’re holding a ROS By-The-Bay meetup at Bosch in Sunnyvale. 

The cat is out of the bag on the OSRA’s new Special Interest Group on Physical AI (SIG PAI). This week the SIG released a fantastic demo repo on Github that shows you how to integrate LeRobot, ROS, and multiple robotic simulators. The SIG PAI also laid out their roadmap for the next few years and how you can get involved.
