Open Robotics has a survey asking for _your_ feedback. Fill it in to enter a prize draw!

[Open Robotics](https://www.openrobotics.org/) works to support the development, distribution, and adoption of open-source software for use in robotics research, education, and product development. As an organisation and as a movement, we know a lot about the software, tools and standards that we build, but _we do not know enough about the people who use them_ in their work, in their hobbies, and in their studies.

What motivates people to use the Open Robotics Suite (ROS, ros-controls, Space ROS, Gazebo, Open-RMF, and the infrastructure that supports them), in whole or in part? What motivates companies to make their products depend on our software? What features are actually relied upon every day, and which are no longer necessary? Or, conversely, what motivates someone to _not_ use the Open Robotics Suite? To begin answering these questions, we are starting a new annual survey of everyone who is or could be using our software.

We are asking anyone and everyone who is in robotics, in any way, to answer this survey. Even if you do not use any Open Robotics software, or you think that ROS is not suitable for your work, or you’ve only just started learning Gazebo, or you tried and have abandoned ROS, we still want to hear from you. If you’ve never used ROS, Gazebo, or any of our other software, we want to hear from you!

Achieving our mission requires knowing who we are or should be building software for, no matter who they are or where they use it. This survey is how we get to know our community better.

**You can fill in the survey by following this link**: [https://go.openrobotics.org/orsurvey26](https://go.openrobotics.org/orsurvey26)

### [](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/ros-pmc-minutes-for-october-13-2026/58975#p-118556-prize-draw-1)Prize draw

All survey participants will have the opportunity to enter a prize draw to win one item of their choice from the [Open Robotics Store](https://store.openrobotics.org/). At the end of the survey you will have the opportunity to enter your email address for the purposes of entering this prize draw. You must enter a valid email address to be eligible for the prize draw. **50 participants will be chosen at random** from those who enter.

### [](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/ros-pmc-minutes-for-october-13-2026/58975#p-118556-deadline-2)Deadline

**The survey period ends on the 30th of November, 2026**. All _completed_ surveys received by this date will be eligible for the prize draw.

### [](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/ros-pmc-minutes-for-october-13-2026/58975#p-118556-who-should-fill-in-the-survey-3)Who should fill in the survey?

_Everyone_! We want the opinions of everyone working in, studying in, and playing in robotics. We want the opinions of every individual - not just one response per company. Get your colleagues to fill it in, your team mates, your staff, your classmates. The more information we have, the better we can work to ensure that the software you depend on better meets your needs - or, if you don’t use it, gets to the point where you want to!

### [](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/ros-pmc-minutes-for-october-13-2026/58975#p-119199-buildfarm-update-3)Buildfarm update

The buildfarm builds and tests ROS packages across all supported distributions and platforms. This section covers new issues, current priorities, and infrastructure changes that affect maintainers.

*   [[@miguelgonrod](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/miguelgonrod)] ![Image 15: :new_button:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/new_button.png?v=15) Issues: nothing new from the buildfarm side.
*   ![Image 16: :warning:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/warning.png?v=15) Priorities: 
    *   ![Image 17: :memo:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/memo.png?v=15) PTAL [Buildfarm Issues Dashboard](https://osrf.github.io/buildfarm-tools/dashboard/index.html) (27 open issues). Several were opened months ago and have had no new information since. Should any of them be disabled?
    *   Build regression in `perf_test`: undefined references to DDS `PubSubType` on Rolling and Lyrical. The root cause appears to be on the Cyclone DDS side.
    *   Segmentation fault in rclpy `test_client` and `test_service`.
    *   `test_launch_ros.pytest.missing_result` intermittently times out, with ClassLoader unload warnings.
    *   `test_load_node_with_param_file` intermittently hangs while loading composable nodes.
    *   `rosidl_typesupport_tests` assume `rosidl_typesupport_cpp` is available.
    *   `tf2_ros_message_filter.multiple_frames_and_time_tolerance_deprecated` is flaky and times out on Rolling and Kilted with Connext.

*   [@miguelgonrod](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/miguelgonrod) will open new issues for the buildfarm items that need attention. [@ahcorde](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/ahcorde) will look into the reported test failures if nobody else picks them up.

## [](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/ros-pmc-minutes-for-october-13-2026/58975#p-119199-agenda-8)Agenda

*   [[@claraberendsen](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/claraberendsen)] [rclpy#1707](https://github.com/ros2/rclpy/pull/1707): 154 mypy regressions in rclpy, caused by generated code, are breaking builds. 
    *   ![Image 25: :memo:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/memo.png?v=15)[@sloretz](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/sloretz) will take a look.

*   [ROSCon feedback](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1CTB9A6pnFRhnir5u6HctQoTmqI8gkh37-HqTEocNrNw/edit?tab=t.0)
    *   Microcontrollers 
        *   ![Image 26: :warning:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/warning.png?v=15) Kat Scott: the microcontroller ecosystem is still fragmented across RMW implementations.
        *   Kimberly McGuire: Sapphire has expressed interest in collaborating on microcontroller frameworks.

    *   macOS support 
        *   Kat Scott: running the ROS 2 and Gazebo tutorials on macOS is still hard.
        *   [@cottsay](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/cottsay) is exploring alternative package managers and looking at a long-term Pixi strategy.
        *   [@claraberendsen](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/claraberendsen): we should define explicit supported use cases and platform boundaries for the other operating systems.

    *   Release docs and vendor experience 
        *   [@sloretz](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/sloretz): vendors are confused about binary releases and installing from apt.
        *   Kat Scott: confusing naming and gaps in the docs make it harder for vendors to adopt ROS.
        *   ![Image 27: :light_bulb:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/light_bulb.png?v=15) Kat Scott proposed improving repository READMEs with documentation and onboarding context.
        *   William Woodall and [@ahcorde](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/ahcorde) discussed using agent markdown files (`AGENTS.md`) to standardize contribution rules across repositories.
        *   ![Image 28: :memo:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/memo.png?v=15) README standardization goes on the agenda for Oct 20, 2026.

    *   Official ROS 2 robot certification 
        *   ![Image 29: :light_bulb:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/light_bulb.png?v=15) Kat Scott proposed a rubric and a formal certification process for official ROS 2 robots.
        *   [@yadunund](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/yadunund): SIG Physical AI is developing a low-cost mobile manipulator.
        *   Andrew Symington suggested test infrastructure and simulated test examples for certified platforms.

    *   ROSCon scheduling and location 
        *   ![Image 30: :light_bulb:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/light_bulb.png?v=15) Kat Scott proposed holding ROSCon every 6 months to allow more preparation time.
        *   [@clalancette](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/clalancette) suggested holding ROSCon right after each release, to plan the year’s priorities.
        *   [@claraberendsen](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/claraberendsen) suggested warm-weather locations for post-release events.

    *   REPs at ROSCon 
        *   ![Image 31: :light_bulb:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/noto/light_bulb.png?v=15)[@ahcorde](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/u/ahcorde) proposed a dedicated track or workshop day to discuss REPs in depth.
