Open Robotics has a survey asking for _your_ feedback. Fill it in to enter a prize draw!

[Open Robotics](https://www.openrobotics.org/) works to support the development, distribution, and adoption of open-source software for use in robotics research, education, and product development. As an organisation and as a movement, we know a lot about the software, tools and standards that we build, but _we do not know enough about the people who use them_ in their work, in their hobbies, and in their studies.

What motivates people to use the Open Robotics Suite (ROS, ros-controls, Space ROS, Gazebo, Open-RMF, and the infrastructure that supports them), in whole or in part? What motivates companies to make their products depend on our software? What features are actually relied upon every day, and which are no longer necessary? Or, conversely, what motivates someone to _not_ use the Open Robotics Suite? To begin answering these questions, we are starting a new annual survey of everyone who is or could be using our software.

We are asking anyone and everyone who is in robotics, in any way, to answer this survey. Even if you do not use any Open Robotics software, or you think that ROS is not suitable for your work, or you’ve only just started learning Gazebo, or you tried and have abandoned ROS, we still want to hear from you. If you’ve never used ROS, Gazebo, or any of our other software, we want to hear from you!

Achieving our mission requires knowing who we are or should be building software for, no matter who they are or where they use it. This survey is how we get to know our community better.

**You can fill in the survey by following this link**: [https://go.openrobotics.org/orsurvey26](https://go.openrobotics.org/orsurvey26)

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/gazebo-pmc-meeting-minutes-2026-09-28/58502#p-118556-prize-draw-1)Prize draw

All survey participants will have the opportunity to enter a prize draw to win one item of their choice from the [Open Robotics Store](https://store.openrobotics.org/). At the end of the survey you will have the opportunity to enter your email address for the purposes of entering this prize draw. You must enter a valid email address to be eligible for the prize draw. **50 participants will be chosen at random** from those who enter.

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/gazebo-pmc-meeting-minutes-2026-09-28/58502#p-118556-deadline-2)Deadline

**The survey period ends on the 30th of November, 2026**. All _completed_ surveys received by this date will be eligible for the prize draw.

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/gazebo-pmc-meeting-minutes-2026-09-28/58502#p-118556-who-should-fill-in-the-survey-3)Who should fill in the survey?

_Everyone_! We want the opinions of everyone working in, studying in, and playing in robotics. We want the opinions of every individual - not just one response per company. Get your colleagues to fill it in, your team mates, your staff, your classmates. The more information we have, the better we can work to ensure that the software you depend on better meets your needs - or, if you don’t use it, gets to the point where you want to!

Hi Gazebo Community!

This week’s meeting was held on September 28, 2026 6:00 PM and the following is a summary:

## [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/gazebo-pmc-meeting-minutes-2026-09-28/58502#p-118579-pmc-business-1)PMC Business

*   **Formal Decision:** Accepted **REP-158: OpenUSD Conventions for Simulation Asset Interoperability in Open Source Robotics**. This was a joint vote with the ROS PMC, and the final results will be announced once officially recorded by both PMCs.
*   A formal vote will be requested to sponsor Cherie Hu as a mentee committer. She has made significant [contributions](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-common/pulls?q=author%3Acheriehu) to `gz-common`.

## [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/gazebo-pmc-meeting-minutes-2026-09-28/58502#p-118579-new-agenda-items-2)New agenda items

*   **ROSCon 2026 Debrief**
    *   **Simulator Landscape & Value Proposition:** Following widespread momentum around MuJoCo at ROSCon (including direct use with `mujoco_ros2_control` and the unveiling of a new renderer), the PMC discussed how to clearly articulate Gazebo’s core value propositions. Key strengths highlighted include ease of use, flexibility, not strictly requiring a GPU, hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) and integration testing, mixed kinematic/dynamic simulation modes, first-class Fuel integration, and the ability to start in Gazebo and export to other simulators via file format converters.
    *   **macOS & Onboarding Feedback:** Feedback from the intro workshop and hackathon emphasized making it easier to get started with Gazebo and investing in first-class macOS support for both Gazebo and ROS—especially with Pixi coming online, as Homebrew currently lacks full ROS integration and many attendees used Mac laptops.
    *   **Zero-Copy Sensor Data Transport:** Inspired by ROSCon sessions on zero-copy `rcl_buffer` image transport, the team discussed using `rcl_buffer` with ROS messages to efficiently expose sensor data from Gazebo to ROS without conversion overhead, with `ros_gz_pointcloud` noted as a prime candidate for integration.
    *   **Hackathon & Future Events:** The ROSCon hackathon was a big success, with participants building Gazebo from source and reviewing pull requests. The team discussed organizing co-located meetings and hackathons at future robotics conferences (such as RSS), noting that hackathons require less preparation overhead than workshops while still being effective for onboarding contributors.

*   **Speeding Up Compilations & Conditional CI Execution**
    *   Discussed skipping unnecessary CI builds when pull requests only modify documentation or text files ([gazebo-tooling/release-tools#1558](https://github.com/gazebo-tooling/release-tools/pull/1558)).
    *   Evaluated moving file-ignore and dependency-graph logic into `gzdev` to intelligently determine which CI builds and tests need to run (accounting for Python bindings and world file changes). While a script in `release-tools` provides an immediate cross-platform fix, prototype dependency-graph tooling has already shown a noticeable reduction in CI runs and will be evaluated further.
    *   Agreed to enable a Jenkins configuration requiring manual approval before running CI on pull requests from new contributors (similar to GitHub Actions) to prevent queue congestion from bulk/automated pull requests.

## [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/gazebo-pmc-meeting-minutes-2026-09-28/58502#p-118579-updates-3)Updates

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/gazebo-pmc-meeting-minutes-2026-09-28/58502#p-118579-buildfarm-ci-discussed-live-4)Buildfarm & CI (Discussed Live)

*   **Ubuntu Resolute Ogre 1 CMake Warnings:**`gz-sensors` CI fails to find `gz-rendering-ogre` 11.0.0 on Ubuntu Resolute ([gazebosim/gz-rendering#1346](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-rendering/issues/1346)) because Ogre 1 components are no longer built in nightlies. Discussion is ongoing in [gazebosim/gz-cmake#587](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-cmake/pull/587) on how to specify components that are required for CI vs. optional for end users without masking silent CI failures.
*   **macOS Sequoia ARM64 GUI Test Failures:**`gz-gui10` Homebrew tests on `mac-six-arm` are failing with `SIGTRAP` when using Qt Metal ([gazebosim/gz-gui#790](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-gui/issues/790)), likely due to XQuartz not running, permission issues, or outdated Xcode CLI tools. The team plans to inspect an idle Jenkins workspace on the host machine.
*   **Windows Flaky Stopwatch Test:** The re-opened `Stopwatch.StartStopReset` test failure on Windows ([gazebosim/gz-math#95](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-math/issues/95)) is occurring roughly every 20–30 builds and may require backporting [gazebosim/gz-math#610](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-math/pull/610).

_Note: The following updates were shared as written updates in the meeting agenda and were not discussed live due to time constraints._

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/gazebo-pmc-meeting-minutes-2026-09-28/58502#p-118579-urdf-sdformat-5)URDF & SDFormat

*   Delivered a ROSCon presentation on URDF 1.1 / 1.2.
*   **In progress:**
    *   Support URDF 1.1 capsules in SDFormat ([gazebosim/sdformat#1722](https://github.com/gazebosim/sdformat/pull/1722)).
    *   Opened a tracking issue to discuss closed-loop kinematics in URDF ([ros/urdfdom#267](https://github.com/ros/urdfdom/issues/267)).
    *   Noted that the `urdfdom` version in `bazel-central-registry` is outdated.

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/gazebo-pmc-meeting-minutes-2026-09-28/58502#p-118579-package-updates-gz-sim-gz-physics-gz-rendering-gz-gui-gz-transport-6)Package Updates (`gz-sim`, `gz-physics`, `gz-rendering`, `gz-gui`, `gz-transport`)

*   **Merged:**
    *   `gz-gui`: Fixed macOS Metal viewport gamma by reinterpreting the sRGB texture view ([gazebosim/gz-gui#783](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-gui/pull/783)).
    *   `gz-physics`: Stored body pointer and constant offset in `FrameInfo` for MuJoCo to speed up load times by removing `site` elements ([gazebosim/gz-physics#1098](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-physics/pull/1098), [google-deepmind/mujoco#3397](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco/issues/3397)).
    *   `gz-physics`: Ensured the MuJoCo arena is properly sized for broadphase collision in static worlds, fixing a crash with `3k_shapes_static.sdf` ([gazebosim/gz-physics#1097](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-physics/pull/1097)).
    *   `gz-sim`: Added Python binding for `ServerConfig::SetSdfString` ([gazebosim/gz-sim#3999](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/pull/3999)).

*   **In review:**
    *   `gz-sim`: Add runtime-typed ECM batch queries and expose them to Python ([gazebosim/gz-sim#4006](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/pull/4006)).
    *   `gz-sim`: Wait for the thruster command before stepping in the thruster test ([gazebosim/gz-sim#4011](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/pull/4011)).
    *   `gz-rendering`: Copy camera frames into caller-owned memory through `PixelBuffer` ([gazebosim/gz-rendering#1344](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-rendering/pull/1344)).
    *   `gz-transport`: Make `CreateMsgFromBuffer` virtual and drop the message factory ([gazebosim/gz-transport#969](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-transport/pull/969)).
    *   `gz-transport`: Drop `enable_shared_from_this` from the Zenoh handlers ([gazebosim/gz-transport#968](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-transport/pull/968)).

### [](https://discourse.ros.org/t/gazebo-pmc-meeting-minutes-2026-09-28/58502#p-118579-packaging-release-tools-documentation-7)Packaging, Release Tools & Documentation

*   **Homebrew & Documentation:**
    *   **Merged:** Renamed `ogre2.3` to `ogre2.3-with-freeimage` ([osrf/homebrew-simulation#3566](https://github.com/osrf/homebrew-simulation/pull/3566)) with corresponding documentation updates ([gazebosim/docs#716](https://github.com/gazebosim/docs/pull/716)), renamed `gz-rotary-ogre2.3-vendor` to `ogre2.3` ([osrf/homebrew-simulation#3567](https://github.com/osrf/homebrew-simulation/pull/3567)), and added a deprecation date to Ionic formulae via script ([osrf/homebrew-simulation#3584](https://github.com/osrf/homebrew-simulation/pull/3584)).
    *   **In review:** Script to disable deprecated Homebrew formulae ([osrf/homebrew-simulation#3583](https://github.com/osrf/homebrew-simulation/pull/3583)).

*   **Release Process & Architecture Support:**
    *   Deprecating `armhf` (32-bit) binary production ([gazebo-tooling/release-tools#1548](https://github.com/gazebo-tooling/release-tools/issues/1548)).
    *   Exploring improvements to reduce release process overhead, including next steps for auto-changelog generation (finishing `release-tools` integration and piloting file collection in `gz-transport15` in [gazebo-tooling/release-tools#246](https://github.com/gazebo-tooling/release-tools/issues/246)) and improvements to the manual Homebrew `build-bottle` step ([gazebo-tooling/release-tools#1560](https://github.com/gazebo-tooling/release-tools/issues/1560)).
